All figures come from \texttt{diagram\_macros.tex}; see the comment block at
its top for the full list of keys. The stand-alone commands
(\verb|\UnitDiskFig|, \verb|\NumberLineFig|, \ldots) each make their own
\texttt{tikzpicture}. The pics (\texttt{ellipse}, \texttt{vector},
\texttt{plane}, \ldots) go inside a \texttt{tikzpicture}, so several
figures can be combined with each other and with ordinary TikZ.

Two things to remember:
\begin{itemize}
  \item A key value that contains a comma or an equals sign must be
        wrapped in braces, e.g.
        \verb|u={2,1}| or \verb|name={$\{a,b\}$}|.
  \item To enlarge a picture that contains pics, use
        \verb|llfig scale=1.5| rather than \verb|scale=1.5|: like nodes,
        pics ignore a picture's \verb|scale|.
  \item 3D pics (\texttt{axes 3d}, \texttt{plane}, \texttt{parallelepiped},
        and \texttt{vector} with three coordinates) need the option
        \verb|llfig view| on the \texttt{tikzpicture}, optionally with a view:
        \verb|llfig view={azimuth}{elevation}|. They then share one view
        with each other and with any plain \verb|\draw (x,y,z) ...|.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Grids at angles and images of grids}

The \texttt{basis grid} pic draws grid lines along any two vectors
$\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}$. With $\mathbf{u} = A\mathbf{e}_1$ and
$\mathbf{v} = A\mathbf{e}_2$ it is the image of the standard grid under
$\mathbf{x} \mapsto A\mathbf{x}$.

\begin{tcblisting}{example, title={A grid rotated by $30^\circ$, with its
    basis vectors}}
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \pic {basis grid={u={0.866,0.5}, v={-0.5,0.866}, vectors,
                    u label=$\mathbf{b}_1$, v label=$\mathbf{b}_2$}};
  \pic {axes 2d={xmin=-2.5, xmax=2.5, ymin=-2.5, ymax=2.5}};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{tcblisting}

\begin{tcblisting}{example, title={A skewed $\mathcal{B}$-grid over the

    2\mathbf{b}_2$}}

\section*{Transforming space by a matrix}

\verb|llfig matrix={a,b}{c,d}| (rows of $A$) on a scope or picture sends
every coordinate $\mathbf{x}$ to $A\mathbf{x}$: grids, axes, vectors,
curves and pics all move together, while text and arrowheads keep their
shape. \verb|\MatrixMapFig| takes the two rows of $A$ and then some TikZ
content, and draws the content before and after. Draw closed shapes with \verb|-- cycle|, not
\verb|rectangle|, and grids with the \texttt{basis grid} pic, not the
\texttt{grid} operation: those two are always page-aligned.

\begin{tcblisting}{example, title={\textbackslash MatrixMapFig: a shear
    moves the whole plane --- grid, axes, disk and vector}}
\MatrixMapFig[before label=$D$, after label=$T(D)$]{1,1}{0,1}{
  \pic {basis grid={xmin=-2, xmax=2, ymin=-2, ymax=2}};
  \pic {axes 2d={xmin=-2, xmax=2, ymin=-2, ymax=2}};
  \filldraw[fill=blue!20, fill opacity=0.6] (0,0) circle (1);
  \pic {vector={to={1,1}, label=$\mathbf{x}$, color=red}};
}
\end{tcblisting}

\begin{tcblisting}{example, title={Fixed axes, transformed content: the
    unit circle and eigenvectors of $A = \left[\begin{smallmatrix} 2 & 1\\
    1 & 2\end{smallmatrix}\right]$ ($\lambda = 3, 1$)}}
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \pic {axes 2d={xmin=-3, xmax=3, ymin=-2.5, ymax=2.5}};
  \draw[thin] (0,0) circle (1);
  \begin{scope}[llfig matrix={2,1}{1,2}]
    \draw[thick, blue] (0,0) circle (1);
    \pic {vector={to={0.707,0.707}, label=$A\mathbf{v}_1$, color=red}};
    \pic {vector={to={-0.707,0.707}, label=$A\mathbf{v}_2$, color=red}};
  \end{scope}
  \node[blue, anchor=west] at (1.6,-1.2) {$A(\text{unit circle})$};
  \node[anchor=west] at (1.6,-1.8) {$A\mathbf{v}_1 = 3\mathbf{v}_1,\
                                     A\mathbf{v}_2 = \mathbf{v}_2$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{tcblisting}
