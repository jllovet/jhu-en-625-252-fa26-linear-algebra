When the vectors have only real components, this definition coincides with the definition from the text.

Let $\vec{x} = \begin{bmatrix}
    2     \\
    1 + i \\
    i
  \end{bmatrix}$ and $\vec{y} = \begin{bmatrix}
    2 - i \\
    2     \\
    1 + 2i
  \end{bmatrix}$, where $i^2 = -1$. Compute the following:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
